HR Salary Bands (Confidential)

Salary bands for FY2026: Junior Engineer 6 to 9 lakh per year, Software Engineer 10 to 16 lakh per year, Senior Engineer 18 to 28 lakh per year, Engineering Manager 30 to 45 lakh per year.

Annual bonus is between 8 and 15 percent of base salary depending on the performance rating. Salary revision discussions happen in April.
