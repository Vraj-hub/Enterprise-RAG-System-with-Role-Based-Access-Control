Company Handbook (Public)

Working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year and 10 days of sick leave.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance. The office is closed on national holidays and on the last Saturday of every month.

The company reimburses travel expenses for approved client visits. Submit receipts within 30 days of the trip.
